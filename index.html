Model the spatial likelihood of landslides across the district.

Landslide Deformation and Displacement Mapping Using SAR Data

Study area
Wayanad, site of the 2024 landslide
Objective
Quantify surface deformation and displacement and assess landslide extent.
Data and methods
Sentinel-1 SAR datasets, with an integrated optical versus SAR analysis to evaluate terrain instability and displacement patterns.
Deliverables
Deformation and displacement maps with landslide extent assessment.

Groundwater Potential Zone Mapping Using AHP

Objective
Identify and classify zones of groundwater potential.
Data and methods
Analytical Hierarchy Process with weighted overlay of seven thematic layers: slope, geomorphology, drainage density, land use and land cover, rainfall, soil and lineament density.
Deliverables
Groundwater potential zone map.
